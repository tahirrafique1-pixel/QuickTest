Privacy Policy for Quick Test by Tahir

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Developer Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use the Quick Test by Tahir mobile application available on Google Play.

2. Permissions & Data Collection

Our application requests minimal device permissions necessary to provide its features:

  • Internet Access: Allows the app to make HTTP/HTTPS API calls and load web content.
  • Network State: Allows app to detect whether device is online or offline.
  • Haptic Feedback / Vibrate: Allows app to trigger device vibration for tactile feedback.
  • Photo Gallery Access: Modern Android 13+ permission to select images from user gallery.

3. Data Storage & Security

We value your privacy. All user preferences and local data are stored securely on your local device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
